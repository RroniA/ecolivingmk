# Service content research

Research date: 5 October 2026. This is an editorial reference for Eco Living; competitor descriptions establish market practice, not independently verified Eco Living equipment or certifications.

## Working assumptions

The client says Eco Living uses the same specifications as local competitors. The revised copy therefore uses SR Servis Logistik as the working reference for vacuum pumping and high-pressure water cleaning, and local suppliers for container size guidance. Availability and actual delivered dimensions remain subject to booking confirmation. The client's statement that the business started in Albania is background information; it does not establish present-day service coverage in Albania or Kosovo.

## Standards and the legal claim

[Albania's DPS catalogue](https://dps.gov.al/sq/project/show/dps:proj:64171) lists SSH EN 840-2:2020 for four-wheel, flat-lid containers up to 1,300 L, with dimension and design requirements. Its catalogue also distinguishes two-wheel, domed-lid, and other container designs. [North Macedonia's standards catalogue](https://isrsm.gov.mk/en/project/list/2?ics=13.030.40&listMode=DEFAULT&operatorCommittee=1&operatorStdType=3&status%5B0%5D=IN_DEVELOPMENT&status%5B1%5D=PUBLISHED) lists MKS EN 840 standards. The Tehnix larger-container catalogue references DIN standards rather than EN 840 for those products.

The sources establish standards and common capacities. They do not verify a universal legal requirement that all businesses use identical external dimensions. Supplier comparisons show differences within the same capacity. No claim that Eco Living is EN-certified or that these measurements are legally compulsory has been added. The full paid standards and all municipal placement rules were not reviewed.

- Updated About service cards and contact choices to include all six services.
- Translated shared service labels and contact form copy. Added no new photographs.
